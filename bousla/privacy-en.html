Fahm Rasikh Privacy Policy

Updated: November 2, 2026

Family account and data

We process the guardian’s email, account identifier and family membership; the student’s chosen nickname, grade and device-link identifiers; lesson images and text, questions, answers, answer-cell images, results, skills, reviews, and service and subscription records. We do not ask for a student email, location or contacts.

Content sharing with your consent

After the guardian explicitly agrees, our server sends necessary content to Anthropic, an artificial intelligence service provider, for four purposes: preparing lesson questions, grading written answers, explaining answers, and preparing premium assistant reports. This includes lesson-page images, extracted text, questions, options and source evidence, answer-cell images and student answers, and performance metrics and skill levels. Selected predefined assistant commands are also sent.

Internal technical identifiers for jobs, source regions, skills and session items accompany the content to associate responses with the correct records. The codes themselves do not contain a name or email. We do not send the student’s name or guardian’s email as separate fields to this provider. Images or text supplied by users may contain personal information: avoid photographing student names or other personal information.

Consent and withdrawal

Neither existing nor new families are opted in by default. The server stores family consent, its timestamp and the text version, and checks it before sending data. Choosing “Not now” leaves content processing disabled; manual lesson creation remains available. Guardians can withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy and deletion. Withdrawal blocks new transmissions; it does not cancel a request already sent. Previously saved data remains available subject to account permissions.

Support messages and email

We process support message content and category, its family-account association, and the guardian’s registered email to respond to questions, troubleshoot issues and handle privacy requests. These are used for app functionality and support, not advertising. Do not include passwords or sensitive images in support messages.

Storage and service providers

Supabase provides authentication, the database and private storage; Anthropic processes the content described above; RevenueCat and Apple manage purchases and subscriptions. Our Supabase project is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany; content processing may occur elsewhere. We do not claim processing within Saudi Arabia. We do not sell family data or use it for advertising tracking.

Retention and deletion

Account and learning records are retained to provide the service until deletion through the app’s supported workflows. Trashed lessons can be restored for 30 days and are then permanently deleted with their files. Guardians can request family deletion in Settings → Privacy and deletion. Device and session access is revoked, and the server processes record and file deletion. Backups may follow the infrastructure provider’s retention cycle. This policy makes no promise about provider model training or provider content-retention duration.

Contact and changes

Use Contact us in the app or the support page for privacy and data requests. We publish the update date and request new consent when a changed sharing notice requires it.
